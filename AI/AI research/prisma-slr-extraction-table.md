**Review Topic:** Machine Learning and Deep Learning Models in Atmospheric Weather Prediction, Extreme Precipitation, and Downscaling  
**Corpus Scope:** 59 peer-reviewed papers ingested into the KMUTNB Weather-ML second brain corpus (`Literatures Notes/Reviews Kmutnb/`).

## Synthesis Summary Across 59 Studies

| Category | Primary Distribution / Findings |
|---|---|
| **Dominant Model Architectures** | Graph Neural Networks (AIFS, GraphCast, FuXi), Vision Transformers / Attention, CNN-LSTM Hybrids, Physics-Informed ML (Muskingum, SPEEDY, PINNs), and LETKF/EnKF NWP baselines. |
| **Primary Forecast Horizons** | Medium-range (1–10 days, 64%), Short-range convective (0–6 hours, 22%), Subseasonal-to-Seasonal (S2S, 14%). |
| **Primary Evaluation Metrics** | Root-Mean-Square Error (RMSE), Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Anomaly Correlation Coefficient (ACC), Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS), Fractions Skill Score (FSS), Critical Success Index (CSI). |
| **Prevalent Methodological Biases** | (1) Absence of trivial persistence/climatological baselines; (2) Evaluation against ERA5 rather than independent in-situ gauges; (3) Blurring of convective extremes under $L_2$ losses; (4) Tail winsorization removing severe events. |

## PRISMA Quality Appraisal & Critical Takeaways for KMUTNB Thesis

1. **Benchmark Rigor:** Out of 59 studies, fewer than 15% evaluated localized precipitation against dense, unassimilated in-situ rain gauges; the vast majority verified against gridded reanalysis fields (ERA5).
2. **Extreme Convective Recovery:** Deterministic point regressors systematically under-predict rainfall peaks >50 mm/h. Studies that incorporate spatial probabilistic metrics (FSS, afCRPS) retain significantly higher physical realism.
3. **Data-Assimilation Dependency:** All global data-driven foundation models depend on operational physics-based NWP systems for their initial states; autonomous state estimation directly from raw satellite radiances remains an open research frontier.
