# ERA5 is ECMWF's fifth-generation reanalysis, spanning 1940 to the present

## Claim

ERA5 is the fifth-generation reanalysis dataset built by the European Centre for
Medium-Range Weather Forecasts under the Copernicus Climate Change Service
program. It records global atmospheric, land, and ocean state continuously from
1940 to the present.

## Evidence

The parent note gives the full name (ECMWF Reanalysis v5), the producing
program (C3S), and the record start of 1940. In the training context, the note
gives the practical window used by neural weather models: 1979 to 2021, that is
42 to 43 years depending on the model quoted.
